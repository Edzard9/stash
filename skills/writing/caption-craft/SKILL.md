# Caption Craft

A caption for real work does not need to shout. The posts this skill
is distilled from present builds, tools, and experiments to a mixed
audience of classmates, builders, and strangers, in Indonesian and
English, and the ones that work share a shape: one concrete thing,
said plainly, with the numbers or the artifact visible. Hype is what
a caption uses when it has no artifact.

## The shape

1. **Hook line, one sentence, concrete.** What exists now that did
   not exist before, or the surprising measured result. Not a
   question to the audience, not "finally released!!".
   - Strong: "Tool ke-4 PerkakasID jadi: kalkulator IPK yang kemarin
     dibangun dua kali cuma buat membuktikan cara kedua 35% lebih
     hemat token."
   - Weak: "Senang sekali akhirnya bisa berbagi project terbaru
     saya!!!"
2. **The middle, 2-4 short lines.** What it does, how it was made, one
   honest limitation or lesson. Specifics over adjectives: sizes,
   counts, times, the stack, the failure that taught something.
3. **The close, one line.** Where to see it (link in bio or the URL
   where the platform allows), or a genuine question the author
   actually wants answered. Never a wall of hashtags; 3-5 relevant
   ones at most, or none.

## Bilingual rule

ID first when the audience is the author's Indonesian community, EN
version below a divider for reach, and the EN is a real rewrite, not
a word-for-word translation: idioms, jokes, and hooks are re-aimed,
not converted. For carousel text, each slide carries one idea, in
large-type-friendly short lines, and the last slide is the artifact
or the link, not a summary of the summary.

## Voice rules (never bend)

- Relaxed and specific. Write like the author talks about their own
  work: proud of the artifact, allergic to overselling it.
- No unnecessary emphasis about the author (how many roles they
  filled, that they worked alone, that it was hard). The work speaks.
- No engagement bait the author would not say out loud: no fake
  questions, no "tag 3 friends", no countdown drama.
- Claims carry their evidence or stay out. A measured number may be
  posted with its measurement; a feeling is phrased as a feeling.
- Emojis: a few, functional ones (a marker, a divider). A caption
  that needs twelve is compensating.

## Variants

- **Build log post:** hook is the finished thing, middle is two
  decisions and one bug, close is the link.
- **Result post (tests, benchmarks):** hook is the number, middle is
  how it was measured and its limits, close is where the full data
  lives.
- **Carousel:** slide 1 is the hook as big text, slides 2-5 one idea
  each, final slide the artifact and where to get it.

## Deliverable

The full caption text, ID and EN, ready to paste, plus the carousel
slide text when asked. Nothing is posted by the agent; publishing is
the author's tap.
